PROFILE

From reporting to production

Md. Sumon Howlader started publishing online in 2014 and built Durbin News into a registered online news portal with a national readership and a large social following. Running a newsroom taught him what an audience gives its attention to and, more usefully, what it scrolls past.

Durbin Films came out of that. The studio produces television drama, web series and brand films for channels, platforms and brands. He works across the studio as producer, from story selection and budget through to final delivery.

He is based between Doha and Dhaka, with the studio and newsroom operating out of Dhaka.
