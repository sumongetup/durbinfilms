THE STUDIO

Durbin Films was founded by Md. Sumon Howlader as a home for stories set in the world our audience actually lives in. We work with writers, directors and actors who want the same thing, on our own productions and on commissions for channels and platforms.

What a client gets is simple: one person to call, a budget agreed before the shoot, and a cut you see before anyone else does.
